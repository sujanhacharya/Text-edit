Local OCR · type-aware editing

Change the words. Keep the look. kept

Rewrite text inside posters, menus, screenshots, scans and phone photos without flattening the design. InkPatch detects each line, keeps its original colour, size, alignment and type style, and repaints only what you change.

  • Fonts, weight, alignment and colour carry over automatically
  • Photos are straightened, evenly lit and filtered before OCR
  • Real text rendered into the pixels — not a blurry patch
  • Runs entirely on this machine, no account, no cloud upload

Designed for real images

Posters. Menus. Photos. Notes.

Tesseract maps every line, then InkPatch samples its foreground colour, typeface family, weight, slant and point size. Phone photos are perspective-corrected and deskewed first, so a page shot at an angle still becomes an editable document. Change a price, rename a title, or erase a caption — the surrounding design stays intact.
